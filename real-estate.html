Sectors · Real estate

Resilient real estate

Hazard-informed site selection, design and portfolio insight, so buildings and developments stay safe, comfortable and valuable.

Get in touch Collaborate with us

Where and how we build determines how exposed people and property will be for decades. Flooding and extreme heat already affect property values, insurance costs and the comfort of residents.

Decisions about sites and designs are often made with limited hazard information, and rarely with a view of how risk will change over a building’s lifetime.

We bring site-specific flood and heat modeling into planning and design, so developers, owners and investors can make resilient choices early, when they cost the least.

Hazards we address

  • Flooding
  • Extreme heat
  • Storms
  • Urban microclimate

Site screening

From weeks to decades

Planning across time horizons

We study environmental extremes from subseasonal and seasonal forecasts to decadal and multi-decadal climate projections, so plans hold up in the coming weeks and in the coming decades.

2 weeks – 2 months

Subseasonal

Weeks-ahead heat and heavy-rainfall outlooks to protect construction sites, residents and building operations.

1 – 12 months

Seasonal

Season-ahead outlooks for flood, hurricane and cooling demand to plan operations and maintenance.

1 – 10 years

Decadal

Near-term hazard trends to inform acquisitions, refurbishment and insurance strategy.

To 2050 and beyond

Multi-decadal

Long-term projections of flooding and heat so new buildings and developments stay safe and valuable over their lifetime.
